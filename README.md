# Baycon Neon Edit

Build a modern, single-page website for Baycon, a professional video editing brand. The site should feel techy and futuristic with neon glitch effects, animated elements, and a red and black color palette — dark background (#0a0a0a), neon red accents (#ff1a1a), and white text. Use subtle glitch animations on headings and neon glow effects on buttons and borders.
Sections to include:

Hero — Full-screen dark section with the "BAYCON" logo in a bold glitchy font, tagline "Your Story. Our Edit. Unforgettable.", and a glowing red CTA button ("See Our Work" / "Get a Quote").
Services — Three service cards with neon-bordered glassmorphism styling:

🎙️ Talking Head Video Editing — clean cuts, captions, color grading for creators & podcasters
🎨 Motion Graphics — animated intros, lower thirds, kinetic text
🎬 Video Editing (General) — reels, ads, YouTube, brand videos


Portfolio — A grid gallery with 6 embed-ready video slots (2 per service category: Talking Head, Motion Graphics, General Editing). Each slot should be a styled iframe container with a red-glowing border, category tag label, and a placeholder embed URL as a comment in the code (e.g. <!-- Replace with your YouTube/TikTok embed URL -->). Use a 16:9 aspect ratio for each video card. Make it easy to swap embed links without touching the design.
About — Short punchy paragraph: "We are The Baycon Team — a video editing brand built for creators and businesses who refuse to blend in. From talking head videos to jaw-dropping motion graphics, we turn raw footage into cinematic, high-impact content that stops the scroll."
Pricing / Packages — Three-tier pricing cards styled with dark glassmorphism and red accents. Add a red "Most Popular" badge on Standard:

Basic — $49: Up to 5 mins edited video, basic color grading, captions, 3-day delivery
Standard — $120 ⭐ Most Popular: Up to 15 mins, color grading, captions, motion graphics intro/outro, 2 revisions, 2-day delivery
Premium — $250: Up to 30 mins, full motion graphics, advanced color grade, sound design, captions, unlimited revisions, priority 1-day delivery


Contact / CTA — Bold section with headline "Ready to Elevate Your Content?" with a contact form: Name, Email, Project Type dropdown (Talking Head, Motion Graphics, General Editing), Message field. Submit button sends to fabulujeomobayonle@gmail.com. Glowing red submit button.
Footer — Minimal dark footer with the Baycon logo, social links:

Instagram: @bayconedit48
TikTok: @bayconedit
Copyright: © 2025 Baycon. All rights reserved.



Design details:

Font: Orbitron or Space Grotesk for headings, Inter for body
Subtle animated grid or scanline background on the hero
Glitch CSS animation on the word "BAYCON" in the hero
Sticky top navbar with smooth scroll
Framer Motion or CSS scroll-reveal animations on each section
Fully responsive (mobile + desktop)
All 6 portfolio video slots must use iframe embeds compatible with YouTube and TikTok embed URLs. Leave clear placeholder comments in the code so the owner can easily swap in real video links. Do not use static images as video placeholders.
